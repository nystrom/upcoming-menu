Privacy Policy for Upcoming

Effective Date: April 28, 2026

Upcoming ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our macOS application, Upcoming.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Calendar Data

Upcoming requires access to your macOS Calendar data to function. This data is accessed locally on your device via the Apple EventKit framework.

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your calendar events to any external servers.
  • All processing of calendar data happens in real-time on your local machine.

1.2 Personal Information

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

1.3 Usage Data

We do not track your usage of the application or collect analytics data.

2. How We Use Information

We use your local calendar data solely to display upcoming events in your menu bar.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Since we do not collect or store your data, we do not share it with any third parties.

4. Security

We rely on the security features of the macOS App Sandbox to ensure that the application only accesses the resources you have explicitly granted permission for.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
